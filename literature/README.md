# Literature / 原始文献索引

Most authoritative sources first; see [machine-readable bibliography](references.csv). **Free access to read ≠ right to rehost a PDF.**

## 1987 field release (primary and measurement papers)
- **Murata, Noguchi et al. (1988)**. *Experimental results of JAERI in the Canadian HT field release study of June 10, 1987.* **JAERI-M 88-232**, official [JAEA PDF](https://jopss.jaea.go.jp/pdfdata/JAERI-M-88-232.pdf), [NDL catalog](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R000000025-I013290002458048). JAEA index describes 83 report pages; official PDF includes front matter and scans of 96 pages. Contents: experimental field/sampling; atmospheric HT and HTO; pine; evaporation; soil HT deposition and soil time histories.
- **Burnham et al. (1988)**. *An Overview of Experiments at Chalk River on HT Dispersion in the Environment.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25295](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25295).
- **Brown et al. (1988)**. *Field Studies of HT Behaviour in the Environment: 1. Dispersion and Oxidation in the Atmosphere.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25296](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25296).
- **Ogram et al. (1988)**. *Field Studies of HT Behaviour in the Environment: 2. The Interaction with Soil.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25297](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25297).
- **Spencer et al. (1988)**. *Field Studies of HT Behaviour in the Environment: 3. Tritium Deposition and Dynamics in Vegetation.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25298](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25298).
- **Jalbert & Murphy (1988)**. *U.S. Team Measurements During the June 1987 Experimental HT Release.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25299](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25299).
- **Noguchi, Matsui & Murata (1988)**. *Tritium Behavior Observed in the Canadian HT Release Study.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25300](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25300).
- **Russell & Ogram (1988)**. *Modelling Elemental Tritium Deposition, Conversion and Reemission Using Ontario Hydro's Tritium Dispersion Code.* [DOI 10.13182/FST88-A25301](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25301).
- **Murata & Noguchi (1997)**. *Dose Delivered by Unit Amount of Tritium Released into the Environment.* JNST 34(2):176–184; [original J-STAGE DOI](https://doi.org/10.3327/jnst.34.176); [open PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnst1964/34/2/34_2_176/_pdf). Table 1 (printed p.177): 1987 five-minute wind/stability/roughness. [Alternate publisher DOI for the **same** article](https://doi.org/10.1080/18811248.1997.9733644).
