# 1987-06-10 30-min HT release — first priority

## Verified protocol
- HT **3.54e12 Bq**, released approximately steadily **15:20–15:50** on **1987-06-10**, from a **1 m point source**.
- A **grassland ~200 m in diameter** and further **~200 m coarse sandy sparse vegetation** formed the experimental field (Brown et al. 1988).
- Atmosphere HT and HTO sampled at multiple downwind distances; [Murata & Noguchi (1997)](https://doi.org/10.3327/jnst.34.176) specifically discusses measurements at **100 m and 400 m**; [Brown et al. (1988)](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST88-A25296) mentions HTO/HT ratios between 5 m and 400 m.
- [Six 5-minute weather observations](../../data/derived/1987_meteorology_5min.csv) from 1997 article, Table 1 (paper p.177), sampled 0.9 m above ground.
- Full original report [JAERI-M 88-232](https://jopss.jaea.go.jp/pdfdata/JAERI-M-88-232.pdf) includes experiment methods and air, soil, pine-needle observations/figures.

**Nominal derived average emission rate** = `3.54e12 Bq / 1800 s = 1.9666667e9 Bq/s` if release was exactly constant; NOT a directly observed source-rate time series.

## Planned validation stages
A. HT plume-only, with source geometry, stability, wind orientation and real sampler averaging interval.  
B. HT soil uptake, oxidation and HTO soil-water profiles.  
C. Soil-generated secondary HTO air plume over days; do not force instantaneous atmospheric HT oxidation.  
D. TFWT and OBT plant response with correct units/plant tissues.  
E. Optionally dose from **measured** concentrations and separately modelled doses.
