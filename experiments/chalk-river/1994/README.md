# 1994-07-27 – 08-08 12-day chronic HT release

Original article: [Davis et al. 1995](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST95-A30509). Continuous HT in air, extensive air/soil/vegetation observations. Soil/air/plant HTO grew toward near steady state, strongly affected by rain; **plant OBT continued increasing** across the experiment.

[Ichimasa et al. 1995](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST95-A30503) describes **four 5m × 5m plots**: one natural, three crops (cherry tomato, radish, Japanese mustard/komatsuna). Plant HTO roughly approached peak around day 10; OBT continued accumulating. Komatsuna leaf OBT/HTO ratio was about **0.06 → 0.24** between exposure days 2 and 11.
