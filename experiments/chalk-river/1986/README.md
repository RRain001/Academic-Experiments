# August 1986 Chalk River HT short-release

**Primary:** Brown, Ogram & Spencer (1990), *Health Physics* 58(2):171–181, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1097/00004032-199002000-00004) · [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2298573/).

From primary abstract: HT in open air did **not** show rapid homogeneous atmospheric oxidation. HTO detected in air was mainly associated with soil HT oxidation followed by HTO reemission. Reported **HT soil deposition velocity**: **0.041–0.13 cm/s**, which is **4.1e-4–1.3e-3 m/s** (derived unit conversion). Average soil HTO loss rate over 21 days **<1% per hour**. Vegetation-water tritium first increased after release, then fell on a time scale similar to soil after ~48 h.

**Secondary history only:** Kim et al. (2012), historical experiment Table 1, lists **1986-08-25**, **15:37–16:07**, **6.85e11 Bq HT**, **1 m above ground point source**. Pending full-text cross-check with Brown (1990), this is NOT an accepted model boundary condition.
